# Names, credit and forks

This page explains how the project's name and the author's credit may be used.
It complements the licence in `LICENSE` and the attribution requirements in
`NOTICE`; it does not replace them.

## The project and its author

"QGIS MCP toolbox" and the Data Explorer, as published at
https://github.com/Adilmunawar/QGIS_MCP, are the work of **Adil Munawar**.
The plugin shows this in its About information and in `get_server_info`.

"QGIS" is a trademark of the QGIS.org association; this project is an
independent plugin and is not endorsed by QGIS.org.

## What you may do

- Use, study and share the software under its licence.
- Fork it, change it and publish your fork, keeping the copyright notices,
  the `NOTICE` file and a clear statement that it is derived from QGIS MCP by
  Adil Munawar, with a link to the original repository.
- Say that your product "works with" or "is based on" QGIS MCP.

## What you may not do

- Remove or hide the author's credit, the `NOTICE` file or the About
  information, or replace them with your own name as if the work were yours.
- Publish a copy or a lightly modified copy under a new name without stating
  its origin, or present it as an original or official release.
- Use the project name, or a confusingly similar one, for a derived product in
  a way that suggests it is the original project or is endorsed by the author.

A fork is welcome when it is honest about where it came from. Taking the work
and claiming it is not.

## Reporting misuse

If you see a copy that strips the credit or claims authorship, please open an
issue at https://github.com/Adilmunawar/QGIS_MCP/issues or write to
adilmunawarx@gmail.com with a link. Copies on GitHub that violate the licence
can also be reported to GitHub under its DMCA process.
